What this guide is about

Why it matters

Leaked content, reposted media, impersonation, and search-result exposure can damage revenue and trust quickly. Acting early gives you a better chance of documenting the issue, finding repeat uploads, and getting the right removal process started.

How 7Ink can help

7Ink White Knight Protection helps creators organise evidence, prepare takedown requests, monitor for copies, and move from a one-off report to a cleaner protection workflow. If this issue affects your content, use the member portal or contact form so the details can be reviewed properly.

Need help with this?

Send the URL, screenshots, and any account details you have. The more exact the evidence is, the faster the issue can be checked.
